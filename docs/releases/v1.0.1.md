The Simpsons port 1.0.1: the players' reports on 1.0.0. Nothing of the game comes in these packages: keep your own
`simpsons.zip` (MAME's ROM set `simpsons`, 4 Players World, set 1) beside the game, and it takes its assets out of
it the first time it starts.

**The names** (issue 3): the console version is **The Simpsons Mr. Plow Edition**, and the arcade game as it is
**The Simpsons Arcade Game**, in every package. What the games keep (settings, high scores, saved states) stays where
it was. Only MAME's `simpsons` set works: its other sets (the 2 Players ones, the Japanese and Asian ones) have
program ROMs of their own, which the decompilation does not cover, and the game says so.

**Nintendo Switch** (issue 2): the games' folder on the SD card is `/switch/simpsons-arcade/`, as 1.0.0's
`/switch/simpsons/` is The Simpsons: Hit & Run's port's too; the games copy their own files from the old folder
once. The Simpsons Mr. Plow Edition no longer draws its picture for 1080p on the 720p screen.

**WIDE** (The Simpsons Mr. Plow Edition):

- Stage 1's start (a player's report): the goon that waits for the opening scene to end no longer stands still in the
  picture's right side; he waits past its edge, as the arcade keeps him past its own, and walks in when the scene
  ends. The enemies that come in at the start of a stage or an area come in from past the picture's edges, and the
  enemies in the picture's sides no longer stop there as if they had reached the screen's edge.
- The picture's sides have the stage's own floor from a stage's start (stage 1's had walls there while its opening
  scene played), so the players and the enemies walk them as in the arcade's 4:3 part.
- Stage 7's leaping ninjas and Krusty's balloon, knocked up in stage 2, bounce off the picture's edges, no longer off
  nothing where the arcade's screen ended; the clown on his ball hops on in the picture's sides.
- Stage 3's climb: the picture no longer jumps sideways as the climb up begins and ends.

**Not tried yet** on a real device: all of the above was checked on a computer, frame by frame where the arcade's
picture and state must not change (the arcade game is the same as 1.0.0's), but not on a Switch, an Android phone or
TV, an iPhone, an Anbernic handheld, a Steam Deck or a Mac. Reports are welcome as
[issues](https://github.com/cheviman/simpsons-port/issues).

The first release of The Simpsons port: Konami's 1991 arcade game *The Simpsons* as a native game, built on its
decompilation ([simpsons-decomp](https://github.com/cheviman/simpsons-decomp)) and checked frame by frame against
MAME in state, picture and sound. Nothing of the game comes in these packages: keep your own `simpsons.zip` (MAME's
ROM set, 4 Players World, set 1) beside the game, and it takes its assets out of it the first time it starts.

**The packages** (Windows, Linux, macOS for Apple silicon and Intel) hold both games and the tools that make the
devices' games with your ROM set:

- **The Simpsons**: the console version. The Konami presentation, the menu on the family's TV, no coins: each player
  picks Marge, Homer, Bart or Lisa on the game's own portraits, and START brings a player in at any time, up to four.
  ARCADE (OPTIONS' lives and continues), MR. PLOW MODE (FREE PLAY with as many continues as you like, BOSS RUSH,
  BONUS GAMES, FRANK GRIMES LIFE), LAN, OPTIONS (CONTROLS for four players, DIFFICULTY, LIVES, CONTINUES, DEMO SOUND,
  SCREEN, VIDEO with PIXELS, WIDE AUTO, 16:10, 16:9 or 20:9 and FPS, VOLUME, SOUND MONO or STEREO), the pause
  (CONTINUE, SAVE STATE, LOAD STATE, RESTART, QUIT GAME; START skips the story scenes instead), the high scores kept,
  Bart's shirt in the show's orange. And a certain code on the menu.
- **The Simpsons Arcade**: the arcade game as it is: coins, the test mode, the EEPROM, four players.
- **Simpsons NSP** (Nintendo Switch: The Simpsons.nsp and The Simpsons Arcade.nsp, with your console's keys),
  **Simpsons APK** (Android: The Simpsons.apk and The Simpsons Arcade.apk, with a key of yours; touch controls or game
  controllers) and **Simpsons Anbernic** (the H700 handhelds' PORTS). The Linux package installs the games in the
  Steam Deck's game mode too.
- The devices' own packages: the Switch's `.nro`s for the Homebrew Menu, the iPhone's and iPad's unsigned `.ipa`s (for
  AltStore, SideStore or Sideloadly), the Anbernic's PORTS folder.

**LAN**: up to four devices on the same local network play one game, a character each: one hosts a room, the others
join it. It is built on [arcade-lan](https://github.com/cheviman/arcade-lan).

**Not tried yet**: a real Switch, an Android phone or TV, an iPhone, an Anbernic handheld, a Steam Deck, a Mac, and
the LAN between two devices. Reports are welcome as [issues](https://github.com/cheviman/simpsons-port/issues).

The game is free, and stays free. To support the decompilations and the ports, and have a say in what comes
next: [patreon.com/c/ChevimanPorts](https://www.patreon.com/c/ChevimanPorts). Entirely optional.

`README.txt` in the package says what each tool needs. What a tool makes is yours alone: never share it.

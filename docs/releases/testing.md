The Simpsons testing: the next release's changes, for the players to try before it comes out. It is not a release:
it is made again at each change, and something in it may not work yet. Tell what you find in the issues
(github.com/cheviman/simpsons-port/issues), with the version The Simpsons Mr. Plow Edition's OPTIONS shows at the bottom right
(V...-TEST...). As in a release, nothing of the game comes in these packages: keep your own `simpsons.zip` (MAME's
ROM set, 4 Players World) beside the game, and it makes its assets out of it the first time it starts.

**To try**

- **WIDE, stage 1's start** (a player's report): the goon that waits for the opening scene to end no longer stands
  still in the picture's right side; he waits past its edge, as the arcade keeps him past its own, and walks in when
  the scene ends. The enemies that come in at the start of a stage or an area come in from past the picture's edges,
  and the enemies in the picture's sides no longer stop there as if they had reached the screen's edge.
- **WIDE, stage 3's climb** (Sebas): the picture no longer jumps sideways as the climb up begins and ends; it stays
  where the play had it.
- **WIDE, the bounces at the arcade's edges**: stage 7's leaping ninjas and Krusty's balloon, knocked up in stage 2,
  bounce off the picture's edges, no longer off nothing where the arcade's screen ended; the clown on his ball hops on
  in the picture's sides.
- **WIDE, the floor in the sides at a stage's start**: the picture's sides have the stage's own floor from the start
  (stage 1's had walls there while its opening scene played), so the players and the enemies walk them as in the
  arcade's 4:3 part.
